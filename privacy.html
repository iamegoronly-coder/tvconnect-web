TVConnect Privacy Policy

Developer: Egor Bazhukov

TVConnect operates under a strict No-Log Policy. We believe your internet traffic is your own business.

What we DO NOT collect:

We do not track, collect, log, or store your browsing history, destination websites, data content, IP addresses, or DNS queries.

What we DO collect:

To provide our service and manage your subscriptions, we only generate and store an anonymous, randomly generated Device Identifier (UUID) and your subscription expiration date. This data is strictly used to authenticate your device with our servers and Apple's StoreKit to grant you access to the VPN network.

We do not require registration, emails, or phone numbers. All payment processing is securely handled by Apple.
